# MINEDROP - A MINIGAMES SERVER - Steal a Mineling

MineDrop server is a self hosted minigames server and it is the first time in Minecraft history to introduce a very new type of minigames

Important words:- [Mineling:- Mobs which are captured] [Players can click the ob, and then money will automatically be deducted from the player’s balance.] [Conveyor belt:- A belt which will starts from one place and moves the mobs spawned at starting to end until it's getting caught or uncatched then removed automatically at the end of belt] [Each player receives a starting balance to buy their first mobs as well as an empty plot of land. ] [Each clan consists of 10 players, and whether it’s a private clan or a public server, everything is synchronized across every server. ]

So MineDrop is a server where you have to make your base and there will be a conveyor belt on which at the starting gate of belt mineling will be spawned at different rarity tiers [COMMON RARE EPIC LEGENDARY MYTHIC BRAINROT] (having difference colors multipliers spawn chance

money generation) and they will be Vanilla + renamed variants mob types for now and after success of the server we will shift to Fully custom texture mobs based on different events etc

The players’ main task is to click the mobs in order to capture these Minelings. Captured Minelings are stored in a separate Mineling inventory, which can then either be placed in their base or sold to the NPC merchant. Every mob has a high sell value depending on its rarity. Since this may become boring after some time, we have several plans for this.

- 1. :- A Destroyer Mineling that spawns randomly and does not have a fixed number of normal Minelings after which it appears, but there is a minimum requirement of 10 normal Minelings, and after at least 10 normal Minelings have spawned it can appear at any time and in any quantity, and before it spawns there are clear warnings including a chat announcement and a siren sound, and when it spawns it randomly selects a player’s base and begins destroying it using a mechanic similar to Endermen breaking blocks but faster and significantly stronger than a normal Enderman, and it does not attack players or mobs but only bases.

- 2. :- Since PvP is not allowed on these servers, there is instead stealth-based gameplay rather than PvP. You can observe other players’ bases and plan a stealth raid to steal Minelings from enemy bases. After a successful theft, you can keep the Minelings (only in solo actions) and place them on your own plot to generate money. You can also place a bounty on specific Minelings, which other players can then steal and return to you in exchange for money. The economy system comes into play afterward. Note: Trading is only possible in the lobby of the public minigame server.

- 3. Players can purchase Minelings from the auction house as well as decorations, building blocks, and tools from traders for their bases in order to protect them from the Destroyer Mineling or from stealth attacks by other players. Trading between players is also available on the server, but direct 1vs1 trades are only possible within the respective clan. Trades outside of a clan are only allowed in the public lobby.

- 4. :- Players can buy protection in exchange for coins, and the protection starts immediately after a successful purchase. On every map, there is an NPC merchant where players can buy decorations, different types of base protection with varying durations, as well as upgrades for their base and various protective tools against Destroyer Minelings. To buy Minelings, however, players must go to the conveyor belt, where different Minelings are continuously transported from the start to the end point. An NPC merchant stands next to the conveyor belt, allowing players to sell Minelings into an auction house that can be seen and accessed by the entire server, whether in a private clan or on the public server, and anyone on the server can also purchase from it.

Now there is a general lobby where you spawn, and only there you can trade with random other players. From the lobby, you can go to two worlds: first, a world called the Minigame Server, whose details are described above, and second, a private minigame world server (also known as the Clan World), in which a maximum of 10 players can play, including the clan leader. It is like a group of friends playing together without interference from the outside world. Now I will explain the private clan world minigame server: every player can create their own clan, which generates a separate world in the main server under that clan’s name, and they can invite up to 9 additional teammates or friends. This world is exactly identical to all features of the main minigame world, but for clarity all details are explained here. When a new player joins the server for the first time, they can enter the public minigame world and play as described. However, if they want to play with friends they met on the world server or invited from other servers, they can create a clan, which creates a separate world in the main server that they can join, similar to the main world server. It is like being a player from India and either joining the international world server to play with players from all over the world, or creating your own clan server named “India” to play only with selected players. Likewise, all clan members can join this private clan world. However, each player can only be in one clan at a time, meaning they cannot be part of multiple clan worlds simultaneously. Whether in the public server or a private clan, a maximum of 10 players can be in each world.
